Machine learning · Biology · Chemistry

Learning representations

I'm Anirban Pal, a PhD researcher at IIT Bombay working across machine learning, biology, and chemistry. I study how models learn useful representations, retain knowledge, and generalize under distribution shift.

My research spans continual learning, self-supervised learning, and JEPA-based world models, alongside biological and molecular representation learning for cancer research, drug discovery, and catalysis.
